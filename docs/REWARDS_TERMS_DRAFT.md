# Rewards/cashback terms — decision draft

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION. OWNER DECISION REQUIRED.**
This records implemented mechanics and missing decisions, not an approved
financial product or redeemable-value promise.

The member submits structured feedback about server-verified purchased dishes.
Server rules determine whether a reward applies and return none/pending/confirmed
with the appropriate integer-qəpik value. The mobile app displays that result
and refreshes one universal wallet. Positive feedback is not required to submit
feedback or a basis for a client-calculated incentive. Phone verification may be
required for reward eligibility under server configuration.

Pending amounts are separate from confirmed balance. A receipt reward hint is
indicative server information, not guaranteed payment. Replaying an already
committed submission must return the original result rather than another reward.
Ledger reversals/expiry, if applicable under approved rules, are server records.

Resolve O3 and API_CONTRACT.md's six decisions before publishing: funder,
restaurant settlement, permitted spending/redemption and step-up authentication,
confirmation/expiry conditions, jurisdictional classification and deletion
treatment. There is no approved withdrawal, transfer, minimum/maximum reward,
percentage, expiry schedule or forfeiture rule in this repository.

Counsel must determine required eligibility, availability, dispute, tax,
termination and consumer disclosures. Do not describe the balance as a deposit,
investment, interest-bearing asset, cryptocurrency or cash equivalent without an
approved legal/business model. No program enrollment or final legal consent is
created by this internal draft.
