# Epsilon terms of service — working draft

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION.** Not binding approved terms. All
entity/contact, governing-law, eligibility, reward and remedy choices refer to
O1–O7 in `LEGAL_TODO.md`; counsel must supply final provisions.

Epsilon supports verified-purchase dish feedback and eligible server-confirmed
incentives. It is not a food-ordering/delivery/reservation service, QR-menu
product or public social review network. Accountless web feedback is future work.

Accounts use the approved phone/Google sign-in mechanisms. Members must control
their login method and avoid sharing credentials or using another person's
purchase reference. Minimum age, minors' consent and geographic eligibility
require approved program/legal rules; the configurable default age is not legal
clearance. Epsilon does not infer account ownership from matching Google email.

Only server-verified purchases and exact returned dishes are eligible for the
implemented feedback flow. Ratings, selected reasons and optional comments must
relate to those dishes. Fraud, fabricated claims, automated abuse and credential
misuse require owner-approved enforcement/moderation rules and review/appeal
procedures. The backend must prevent duplicate claims and rewards. This draft
does not invent penalties, automatic forfeiture or suspension appeal periods.

An incentive is not a prerequisite for feedback. Reward eligibility, amount and
status come from the server and approved program terms. A pending amount is not
confirmed balance; a hint is not a guarantee. One universal wallet is displayed.
Funding, settlement, spending, confirmation, expiry and deletion consequences
remain open decisions; no cash withdrawal, bank deposit, investment return,
interest, transferable value or guaranteed entitlement is represented here.
See `REWARDS_TERMS_DRAFT.md`; do not treat server configuration alone as a legal
substitute for approved, disclosed program rules.

Connectivity and service interruptions can delay verification or delivery.
The app may retain bounded pending feedback and retry with the same key; a
missing response does not prove a write failed. Authoritative server records
determine results. Support/dispute handling, service commitments and remedies
require owner/counsel approval; there is no invented uptime or payout promise.

Profile/privacy settings include backend account deletion after fresh
authentication and confirmation. Actual server deletion, retained records and
pending balances are unverified and must be settled under approved retention/
program rules. Do not describe token removal as account deletion or promise
immediate erasure of all financial records.

Publisher/restaurant intellectual property and logo rights require permission.
User comment licenses, permitted uses, moderation and restaurant access must be
defined with privacy protections and legal review. No ownership transfer or
broad perpetual content license is invented by this draft.

Counsel must draft any liability limits, warranties, termination, dispute law/
forum, consumer protections, change-notice process and contact/effective-date
terms appropriate to the actual operating model. Nothing here claims legal,
store or regulated-financial approval.
